# Portfolio

A personal portfolio with an interactive planetary scene above the projects.

## Language

**Planetary scene**: The interactive space view accompanying the profile. It has a compact mode with one planet and a full mode with planetary systems and optional space effects.
_Avoid_: Map, background video.

**Planetary system**: A selected planet together with its moons and rings, when present.
_Avoid_: Texture (which describes only its surface image).

**Planet selector**: The controls for choosing a planetary system and toggling space effects.

**Space effect**: An optional phenomenon, such as meteors or a distant sun, that remains selected when the planetary system changes.
